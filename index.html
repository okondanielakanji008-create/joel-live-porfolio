How I build

The two things that actually matter once an automation is live and unsupervised.

Idempotent by design

The same trigger firing twice never creates two records. This is structural, built into the scenario logic — not a fix applied after you notice duplicates.

Failures get logged, not lost

Every scenario includes error-handling routes. If something breaks, you get a clear log of what happened — not a silent gap in your data.

Documented, not dependent

Every build ships with documentation and a walkthrough, so your team can maintain it without needing me indefinitely.

Honest scoping

I'll tell you if it's a quick fix or a full rebuild before you spend anything — not after.
